Why Selangor Employees Get Extra Long Weekends

Malaysia's public holiday system has two layers: persekutuan (federal, gazetted by the Prime Minister's Department, observed in all states) and negeri (state, gazetted by each state government). For 2026, Selangor gazettes 5–6 state holidays in addition to the 17 federal ones. The ones that materially affect long weekends are:

  • Thaipusam — falls Sun 1 Feb 2026 in Selangor. State gazette declares Mon 2 Feb as the replacement holiday, creating a 3-day free stretch (Sat 31 Jan – Mon 2 Feb) that federal-only calendars don't show.
  • Hari Keputeraan Sultan Selangor — gazetted on Fri 11 Dec 2026. A natural 3-day weekend (Fri 11 – Sun 13 Dec). 1 AL on Mon 14 Dec gives a 4-day stretch.
  • Awal Muharram — gazetted in Selangor the same day as federal (Wed 17 Jun 2026). Check the Selangor state gazette to confirm.
  • Nuzul Al-Quran — also tied to federal gazette in most years.

By comparison, Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu do not gazette Thaipusam as a public holiday (Kelantan in particular has its own set of Islamic observance days that replace Thaipusam). If you're comparing Selangor vs. Kedah, you typically gain 1 extra long weekend per year from state holidays alone.

Edge case — Federal Territory Day (1 Feb): This is a public holiday in Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and Labuan, but not in Selangor. So if your office is in Petaling Jaya, Shah Alam or any Selangor location, 1 Feb is a normal working day. If you cross the border to KL or Putrajaya, you get it off. Check the office location, not the company HQ, when confirming.

Recommended AL Plan for a Selangor Employee in 2026

With 14 AL days and the Selangor calendar, the optimal plan is similar to the federal plan but with 2–3 additional state-only entry points. The recommended priorities:

  1. Free (0 AL): 30 May – 2 Jun (4 days), 31 Jan – 2 Feb (3 days Selangor-only), 20 – 23 Mar (4 days), 7 – 9 Nov (3 days), 11 – 13 Dec Sultan Selangor (3 days Selangor-only), 17–18 Feb CNY (2 days).
  2. 1 AL high efficiency (5×): Fri 29 May OR Wed 3 Jun (5 days off around the May–Jun cluster), Thu 19 Mar (5 days for Hari Raya), Mon 16 Feb (5 days for CNY). Add the 4× Deepavali bridge (Fri 6 Nov → 4 days) and the Selangor-specific Mon 14 Dec (4 days around Sultan Selangor's Birthday).
  3. 2 AL medium efficiency: For the Thaipusam stack, take Thu 29 Jan as 1 AL to extend 31 Jan – 2 Feb to a 4-day stretch (29 Jan – 2 Feb). Use remaining AL for block leave.

The 14-AL plan should give a Selangor employee around 60–65 total days off in 2026 (counting weekends), versus the ~50 a federal-only employee would get. The 10-day gap is largely driven by the Thaipusam cluster and the Sultan Selangor's birthday.

Selangor-Specific FAQ

What extra long weekends do Selangor employees get in 2026?
The headline Selangor-only long weekend in 2026 is 31 Jan – 2 Feb 2026 (3 days) for Thaipusam + Thaipusam replacement holiday. Thaipusam falls on Sunday 1 Feb, with the substitute holiday on Monday 2 Feb, so the stretch runs Sat 31 Jan – Mon 2 Feb. This stack is invisible on federal-only calendars and only applies in Selangor, Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya, Negeri Sembilan, Perak and Johor (Thaipusam is a gazetted holiday in these states but not in Kedah, Kelantan or Terengganu). KL/Putrajaya/Labuan additionally get Federal Territory Day, extending the same window to a 4-day stretch (Sat 31 Jan – Tue 3 Feb).
When is the Sultan of Selangor's Birthday 2026?
The official birthday of DYMM Sultan Selangor is 11 December, and in 2026 the gazetted public holiday (Hari Keputeraan Sultan Selangor) falls on Friday 11 December 2026 — a natural 3-day weekend (Fri 11 – Sun 13 Dec) with zero AL. Extend it to a 4-day stretch with 1 AL on Monday 14 Dec.
Does Selangor observe Thaipusam as a public holiday?
Yes. Selangor is one of the states that gazettes Thaipusam as a state public holiday (others: Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya, Negeri Sembilan, Perak, Penang, Johor). Kedah, Kelantan and Terengganu do not observe Thaipusam as a public holiday.
Is Federal Territory Day a holiday in Selangor?
Federal Territory Day (Hari Wilayah Persekutuan) on 1 February is a public holiday in Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya and Labuan only — it is NOT a public holiday in Selangor state. If you work in Selangor (not in KL/Putrajaya), you do not get 1 Feb off unless your company grants it as a special rest day.

Source & accuracy: Selangor state holiday data is sourced from the Pejabat Setiausaha Kerajaan Negeri Selangor (selangor.gov.my), cross-referenced with the federal Prime Minister's Department gazette. Important: Islamic holiday dates (Hari Raya Aidilfitri, Hari Raya Haji, Maal Hijrah, Israk & Mikraj, Maulidur Rasul) are determined by moon sighting and may shift ±1–2 days from the preliminary schedule. Thaipusam dates depend on the Tamil calendar and are gazetted by individual state governments. Always verify the final gazette at kabinet.gov.my and your state's official portal before booking travel or applying for leave.
