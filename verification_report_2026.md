### Key Findings

- **All primary state holiday dates are 100% correct** — every Sultan birthday, Governor birthday, Thaipusam, Israk and Mikraj, Awal Ramadan, Arafat Day, Good Friday, Harvest Festival, Hari Gawai, Sarawak Day, Georgetown Day, Installation of Sultan Terengganu, Independence Declaration Day, Christmas Eve, and YDPB Negeri Sembilan date matches the official JPA PDF exactly.

- **All substitute/"Holiday" entries follow correct cascading substitution rules** — when a holiday falls on a weekend, the substitute is correctly pushed to the next available working day, accounting for other holidays that may fall on the same substitution chain.

- **2 entries could not be verified** against the official JPA gazette but are corroborated by publicholidays.com.my. These should be manually reviewed against the actual state gazettes (kedah.gov.my, sarawak.gov.my) if available.

- **3 classification issues** (federal vs. state type) were noted for Nuzul Al-Quran and Hari Raya entries — dates are correct but the "type" field may be mislabeled.
